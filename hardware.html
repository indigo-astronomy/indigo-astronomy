Device drivers and agents distributed with INDIGO 3.0-3

INDIGO 3.0-3 comes with 135 device drivers and 10 agents. The icons show on which platforms each of them is available: Linux, macOS and Windows.

Please note that INDIGO for Windows is not released yet, this is why the Windows icons are greyed out. The drivers marked with are already ported and they will be available when the Windows version is released.

To check whether your device is supported by INDIGO, look for its driver in the list of device drivers below.

The list of device drivers distributed with INDIGO 2.0 is available here.
